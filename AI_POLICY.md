# AI / LLM Contribution Policy

DeckCheatz is a community project, and we'd like to keep this practical rather
than political. This policy isn't a judgment on AI tools one way or the
other — it's here to keep contributions and reviews genuine, whatever tools
you used to get there.

## LLM-assisted contributions are welcome

Using an LLM to help write code, docs, commit messages, or issue reports is
fine. What matters is that you understand what you're submitting and stand
behind it — not how you arrived at it.

## A human has to be in the loop

Every LLM-assisted contribution must be driven and reviewed by a person at
each step. We don't accept agentised submissions — for example, a bot that
opens issues or PRs, or replies to review feedback, on its own without a
human reading and approving first. If an LLM helped write something, a human
still needs to have read it, understood it, and decided to send it.

## Please respond to reviews yourself

When a maintainer leaves review comments, we'd politely ask that you reply in
your own words, even if an LLM helped you get there. Review is a
conversation, and it works best when it stays one.

## We use AI tooling too

This isn't a one-way rule. Maintainers use LLMs as part of our own workflow
as well, including for code review, under the same human-oversight principle
above — someone is always reading and deciding before anything lands.
